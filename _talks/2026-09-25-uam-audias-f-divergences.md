**Talk:** *Approximating f-Divergences with Rank Statistics*.

Invited seminar at the **AUDIAS (Audio, Data Intelligence and Speech)** group, **Escuela Politécnica Superior, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid**. The talk presents a rank-statistic approach to approximating **f-divergences directly from samples**, without explicitly estimating densities or density ratios, together with convergence guarantees, multivariate extensions based on random projections, and applications to generative modeling. Joint work with **Viktor Stein**.

**Friday, September 25, 2026 · 11:00 · Lab C-109**, Escuela Politécnica Superior, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid.

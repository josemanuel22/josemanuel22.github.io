**Spotlight oral presentation** at AISTATS 2026, given in **Oral Session 6: Generative Modeling (Diffusion / Flow / Temporal / Graph)**. The talk presented dual-ISL, a rank-based likelihood-free objective that induces convex optimization over model densities, admits an explicit Bernstein-based density approximation, and extends to multivariate distributions through slicing.
